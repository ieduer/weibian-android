The project owner explicitly confirmed on 2026-07-29 that the project has
authorization to redistribute the Yang Bojun *Lunyu Yizhu* translation and
annotations through the public Android APK and the project content Worker.

This repository records only the operational attestation. Contract documents,
licensor contacts, payment records and other private legal material remain in
the owner's approved private records and must not be committed to this public
repository.

## Release boundary

- The MIT `LICENSE` covers code only.
- The corpus is excluded from public source control but may be present in
  signed release APKs and the authorized production content service.
- A new source edition, translation, commentary, image or audio corpus requires
  its own rights review; this receipt does not automatically extend to it.
- If the owner withdraws or cannot renew the authorization, publication stops
  fail-closed: freeze content pointers, remove public download promotion, retain
  evidence, and publish a higher-version repair containing only approved
  content. Do not overwrite immutable release objects.

## Verification

At every release, the operator confirms:

1. the content still belongs to the authorized corpus and scope;
2. public source archives still exclude generated corpus files;
3. APK, Worker content manifest and release notes identify the same content
   version;
4. the owner has not revoked this attestation.
